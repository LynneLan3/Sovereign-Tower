Sovereign Tower beginners guide: where to start

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This beginners guide sticks to the gameplay loop described on the official Steam page. It is not a spoilery walkthrough and does not invent optimal builds, hidden quests, or ending routes that official store materials do not document.

Primary source: Steam “About This Game” for Sovereign Tower (Wild Wits Games / Curve Games).

1. Understand the fantasy

You are the Sovereign of a magical tower. Your job is to rule: hear petitions, make choices, manage a Round Table of Knights, and shape the kingdom’s destiny. If things go wrong, official materials say you can turn back time and try again with knowledge retained.

2. Learn the daily loop

Steam’s advisor framing (Arlin) describes a day in the life of a Sovereign:

  1. Morning: tend to subjects and listen to kingdom problems
  2. Decide: each choice affects relationships and your legacy
  3. Balance affinity with Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles, and the People — and the Treasury
  4. Use the Round Table to assign Knights to the requests you accepted
  5. Review quest results (including critical success, failure, or unexpected outcomes)

3. Match Knights to quests

Knights have five stats listed on Steam: Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, and Wit. Quests need different skill sets. Official guidance is simply to assign the Knight best equipped for the job to reduce chances of failure.

Steam also notes that Knights have strong personalities and that keeping them satisfied matters — otherwise they may leave your court.

4. Expand the tower when you can

Annexes open more court positions and crafting support. Official examples include Carina’s Forge and the Witch’s Alchemy Room. Exact unlock order and costs are not fully detailed on the store page.

5. Use time rewind as a learning tool

A Demon in the Crypts can turn back time. Official copy frames this as keeping knowledge and making alternate decisions — useful for beginners experimenting with court choices and quest assignments.

6. Romance exists — but start with the court loop

Romance is part of the official pitch (Knights and the Lady of the Tower). For what is confirmed versus inventable fan lists, see romance options. New players can focus first on decisions, factions, and quest assignment.

Quick checklist

  • Play on the confirmed PC / Steam platform
  • Read the fuller gameplay overview
  • Assign Knights by stat fit, not only story preference
  • Treat time rewind as an intended system, not a “cheat”
  • Do not expect console editions until officially announced

FAQ

How do you start Sovereign Tower as a beginner?

Follow the official loop: hear subjects, make decisions, recruit and assign Knights from the Round Table, then use time rewind if you need to revisit choices.

What should new Sovereign Tower players focus on?

Matching Knight stats to quests, watching court satisfaction, and learning how faction / Treasury choices reshape the kingdom — as described on Steam.
