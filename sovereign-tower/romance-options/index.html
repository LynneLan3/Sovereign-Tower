Does Sovereign Tower have romance options?

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Yes. Official Steam marketing for Sovereign Tower explicitly includes romance. This page answers the search question using only what the store page states — it does not invent a full romance system guide.

What official sources confirm

On the Steam “About This Game” section, Curve Games / Wild Wits materials state that you can:

  • Pursue forbidden romances as part of bending time and tempting fate
  • Explore romance with Knights alongside advice and grievances at court
  • Speak to the Lady of the Tower — Steam jokes about “romancing stone statues” and points players to her

Relationships more broadly are also part of the pitch: decisions affect relationships with individuals, collectives, and other rulers.

What is not confirmed here

The Steam store page does not publish a complete romanceable-character roster, affinity thresholds, gift lists, or ending charts. Those details are therefore marked as not confirmed on this site and are not filled in for SEO.
  • Full list of every romanceable character — not published as an official catalog on Steam
  • Romance endings / multiple endings tied to each partner — not listed on the store page
  • Step-by-step romance walkthroughs — not official store content

How this relates to gameplay

Romance sits inside the wider court-management fantasy described on Steam: recruit Knights, manage egos, assign quests, and optionally rewind time. For the broader loop, see the gameplay overview and beginners guide.
