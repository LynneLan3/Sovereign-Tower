About Sovereign Tower

Sovereign Tower is a story-rich Round Table management RPG from developer Wild Wits Games and publisher Curve Games. You play as the Sovereign of a magical tower: recruit eccentric Knights, assign quests, manage court drama, expand the tower, and—if outcomes go wrong—turn back time to uncover secrets and try again.

Steam lists the game under Indie and RPG. The developer site also describes it as Narrative / RPG / Visual Novel and as a solo experience. Official launch announcements confirm a PC release on August 6, 2026.

Gameplay

Official Steam materials describe a day-to-day reign loop: hear subject problems, make decisions that affect factions and relationships, recruit Knights with stats (Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic, Wit), assign them from the Round Table to quests, then review results ranging from critical success to unexpected outcomes. You can expand the tower with annexes, craft support items, and use a Demon in the Crypts to rewind time with knowledge retained.

Current platforms

Official Curve Games and Steam launch communications confirm PC availability. The Steam store page publishes Windows and SteamOS + Linux system requirements. The Wild Wits developer page also lists Windows / macOS.

PlayStation 5, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, and mobile versions are currently unconfirmed in those official sources. Searching for “Sovereign Tower PS5” or “console” does not mean a console edition has been announced.

FAQ

What is Sovereign Tower?

A story-rich Round Table management RPG by Wild Wits Games, published by Curve Games. You rule from a magical tower, manage Knights and quests, and can turn back time to rewrite fate.

When did Sovereign Tower release?

August 6, 2026 on PC, per the Steam store page and Curve Games launch announcements.

Is Sovereign Tower on PS5 or other consoles?

Official materials confirm PC via Steam. PS5, Xbox, Switch, and mobile releases are currently unconfirmed.

Does Sovereign Tower have romance options?

Yes — the Steam description explicitly mentions forbidden romances, romance with Knights, and the Lady of the Tower. Detailed romance routes and endings are not fully listed on the store page. See the romance options page.

Who developed and published Sovereign Tower?

Developed by Wild Wits Games; published by Curve Games.
