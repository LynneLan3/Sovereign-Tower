## About Sovereign Tower

**Sovereign Tower** is a story-rich Round Table management RPG from WILD WITS GAMES and Curve Games. The available official descriptions place the player in charge of a magical tower, recruiting eccentric Knights, assigning quests, balancing court relationships, and using time rewind to revisit outcomes.
