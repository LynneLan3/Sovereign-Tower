## Where should beginners start?

The safest source-backed starting point is to understand the game's ruling loop before looking for detailed route advice. Sovereign Tower's official descriptions identify the tower, the Round Table, Knights, quests, court relationships, annexes, and time manipulation as the core pieces to learn.

## A practical first pass

1. **Learn the tower context.** The tower is the management center for the game’s magical court.
2. **Recruit and assign Knights.** Official descriptions connect Knights with the Round Table and quest system.
3. **Watch relationships.** Court relationships matter to the story-rich management loop, but the sources do not publish a complete route chart.
4. **Expand with annexes.** The demo description identifies tower expansion as part of the early loop.
5. **Use time manipulation deliberately.** The game’s time-rewind premise allows outcomes to be revisited; exact unlock rules are not documented in the reviewed sources.

This is orientation rather than a quest walkthrough. It avoids inventing names, unlock conditions, optimal builds, or endings.
