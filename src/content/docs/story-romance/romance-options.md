## Does Sovereign Tower have romance options?

Yes. The official Steam description confirms **forbidden romances**, including romance involving Knights and the **Lady of the Tower**.

## What is not confirmed?

The available official sources do not publish a complete list of romanceable characters, route requirements, choice consequences, or endings. This page therefore confirms the existence and framing of romance without presenting an invented route checklist.

For the broader relationship and Round Table systems, see [Sovereign Tower gameplay](/sovereign-tower/gameplay/). For beginner orientation, see the [beginners guide](/sovereign-tower/beginners-guide/).
