## What is Sovereign Tower gameplay like?

Sovereign Tower is described as a story-rich **Round Table management RPG**. The confirmed loop centers on ruling a magical tower, recruiting eccentric Knights, assigning quests, balancing court relationships, expanding the tower through annexes, and using time manipulation to revisit outcomes.

The official demo and store descriptions support this high-level systems overview. They do not provide enough stable information for a complete character database, quest walkthrough, build guide, or ending guide.

## Confirmed systems

- Round Table and court management
- Recruiting and managing Knights
- Assigning quests
- Expanding the tower with annexes
- Relationship and romance context
- Time rewind or time manipulation to revisit outcomes
