# Sovereign Tower SEO research

Research date: 2026-08-11

## Confirmed identity and facts

- **Developer / publisher:** WILD WITS GAMES / Curve Games.
- **Release:** the Steam Community launch announcement states the full game is out on August 6, 2026 for PC via Steam.
- **Game identity:** a story-rich Round Table management RPG in which the player rules a magical tower, recruits eccentric Knights, assigns quests, balances court relationships, and can rewind time to revisit outcomes.
- **Romance:** the official Steam description confirms forbidden romances, romance involving Knights, and the Lady of the Tower; it does not publish a complete romanceable-character or ending catalog.
- **Platform evidence:** PC/Steam is confirmed. The reviewed official Steam and publisher materials do not confirm PS5, Xbox, Switch, or mobile editions. Steam’s launch communication also announced Steam Deck Verified status.

Those topics now have player-generated community material, but the factory’s public factual layer remains limited to claims supportable by official store, publisher, developer, and launch sources.
